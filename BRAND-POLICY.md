The Naut name, logo and wordmark identify the official product maintained by Second Shift (@secondshift-dv). Source licensing does not grant a trademark license or imply endorsement.

The official public repository is https://github.com/secondshift-dv/naut. Official binaries, signed update metadata and downloads are distributed only through that repository's GitHub Releases. The private naut-dv repository is the engineering authority.

Forks and unofficial distributions must use a distinct product name and visual identity. Do not use Naut branding to imply that a modified build, competing product, mirror or community upload is an official Naut release. State the relationship to Naut clearly and preserve applicable copyright and license notices.

Factual references, reviews, tutorials and community discussion may identify Naut by name and link to the official repository. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Community channels, including Discord, are promotion and discussion surfaces, not official binary authorities.
