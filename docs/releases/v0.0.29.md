# Naut v0.0.29

## Highlights

- Opening an affected Figure queues a targeted ModelRender refresh. The previous valid derivative stays available while Naut prepares its replacement. Open Figures keep their camera position.
- Presentation Contract 3 gives pack authors separate Spotlight artwork, identity, and pagination regions, responsive Spotlight and Banner layer placement, and separate wide and narrow sizes for Figure regions.
- Home keeps **Show Recently** when its active layout pack is removed or replaced.

## Download and update

Download [Naut v0.0.29 for Windows x64](https://github.com/secondshift-dv/naut/releases/download/v0.0.29/naut-v0.0.29-win-x64.zip). Extract the complete archive and run `naut.exe`. Existing users can update through Settings → About.

## Notes

Existing Contract 1 and 2 packs are supported. Packs using the new composition options require Contract 3. Newly prepared and refreshed NFIG derivatives use a format readable by v0.0.27. A derivative generated by v0.0.28 becomes compatible once Naut has refreshed it. Original media and thumbnails are preserved during this refresh.
